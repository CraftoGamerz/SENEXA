# Frontend and presentation guidelines

Product uses black/orange #ff5a00, silver text, Space Grotesk / DM Sans / JetBrains Mono. Preserve the app in screenshots and visibly label synthetic values.

Deck follows official template: 10 x 5.625 inch 16:9, exact headings and sponsor branding, original illustrated cover. Retain original Google Sans where present. Body content occupies existing open area below the heading. White/near-white canvas with ink text and restrained orange accents matching template autumn theme. Prioritize legibility, native diagrams, compact readable table, consistent outline vector icons. Align content edges and retain generous separation. No emojis or invented charts. Essential explanations stay on slide. PDF and PPTX must match.

Use real proportional screenshot crops with callouts. Normal reading order: required topic, takeaway, main visual, support. Minimum supporting text approximately 12pt on this 10-inch canvas, body typically 14-18pt. Links have descriptive labels. Color never carries state alone.

## Phase 6 SaaS design
Off-white #f5f6f8 canvas, white panels, ink #17212f, muted #687386, indigo #5555e7 primary and warm orange SENEXA logo. Dark mode uses navy surfaces. DM Sans body, Space Grotesk headings; bundled fonts. 8px spacing rhythm, 14-20px corners, subtle borders, minimal shadows. Desktop 232px sidebar, top command/search bar, 4 metrics, main telemetry chart and asset risk panel. Responsive collapse under 1000px, mobile drawer under 700px, no body horizontal overflow. Lucide icons only. Native labelled controls, visible focus, modal focus management/escape, status text plus color, reduced motion. Distinguish data source with persistent banner and freshness indicators. Avoid em dashes in product copy.


Expansion: animated onboarding uses CSS keyframes and honors reduced motion. Pairing is prominently identified as a demo. Technical glossary uses keyboard-accessible help controls. Export menu distinguishes CSV records from Markdown interpretation context. Keep the calm indigo/off-white SaaS design and add statistics from actual available readings, not invented business savings.
